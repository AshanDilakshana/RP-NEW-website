Student Researchers · Group R26-IT-132

Individual Research Contributors

Four final-year undergraduate researchers, each responsible for an independent meteorological parameter and seasonal subsystem:

Subsystem: Temperature & Pressure (Northeast Monsoon)

Sankadeepa W.D.I.

Registration No: IT22113986

Research Contribution: Designed and implemented the Hybrid Random Forest + LSTM architecture for short-term (T+3h) surface temperature and barometric pressure forecasting (Hybrid R² = 0.84, MAE ≈ 0.86). Developed the aeronautical translation layer computing Pressure Altitude and Density Altitude categories (Normal, Elevated, Critical) for runway takeoff weight and climb rate planning.

it22113986@my.sliit.lk Individual Thesis (Drive) ↗
Subsystem: Wind Patterns & Pilot Portal (Southwest Monsoon)

Ranasinghe R.A.A.D.

Registration No: IT22269720 · Ashan Dilakshana

Research Contribution: Developed the wind prediction engine utilizing cyclic sine/cosine trigonometry transformations, XGBoost (1-hour nowcast), and Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT 3-hour forecast), achieving wind speed MAE < 1.00 knot (well within ICAO tolerance). Implemented Runway 04 vector resolution for headwind/crosswind calculation, crosswind alert logic, and the pilot decision-support portal.

it22269720@my.sliit.lk Individual Thesis (Drive) ↗
Subsystem: Cloud & Visibility (First Inter-Monsoon)

Jayawardhana H.M.S.I.

Registration No: IT22619976

Research Contribution: Engineered the cloud coverage classification and runway horizontal visibility estimation models using Gradient Boosted Trees (XGBoost), achieving 78.15% cloud accuracy and reducing visibility MAE by 28.3% compared to baseline Decision Trees. Built feature pipelines incorporating dew-point depression and wind-pressure interactions, serving predictions via a lightweight API.

it22619976@my.sliit.lk Individual Thesis (Drive) ↗
Subsystem: QNH, Dew Point & RH (Second Inter-Monsoon)

Jayathilaka P.K.V.K.

Registration No: IT22069504

Research Contribution: Designed and deployed the barometric pressure (QNH) and dew point forecasting pipeline using LightGBM, coupled with the August-Roche-Magnus thermodynamic formula to derive Relative Humidity while preserving thermodynamic equilibrium (Dew point MAE: 0.58 °C, QNH MAE: 1.8 hPa). Implemented a localized radiation fog early-warning rule.

it22069504@my.sliit.lk Individual Thesis (Drive) ↗
